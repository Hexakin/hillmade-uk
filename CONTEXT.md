# hillmade.uk

The public reading home for Jonathan Hill's novel in public: draft chapters of the current book, and the notebook recording how the book is being made.

## The book

**Manuscript**:
The author's master copy of a chapter, kept in the Novel repo (`WRO/chapters/`). The site never edits it.
_Avoid_: source, original

**Chapter**:
A published copy of one manuscript chapter as it appears on the site, with a number, title, description and version.
_Avoid_: episode, instalment, post

**Typeset text**:
A chapter's text as readers see it, with typographic punctuation (curly quotes, proper dashes and ellipses) applied automatically. It differs from the manuscript only in typography, never in wording.
_Avoid_: formatted text, clean copy

## Change over time

**Version**:
A numbered public state of a chapter (v1, v2 and so on). This is the only change-tracking word readers see.
_Avoid_: edition, draft number

**Revision**:
The act of publishing a new version of a chapter.
_Avoid_: edit, update, new edition

**Superseded version**:
An earlier version of a chapter, frozen exactly as it was published and kept readable at its own permanent address.
_Avoid_: old draft, archived version

**Revision note**:
The public explanation that comes with a revision, saying what changed and why.
_Avoid_: changelog, release note

## The notebook

**Notebook**:
The dated public record of the book's making: excerpts, characters, lore, decisions and changes. "Archive" is an internal word only; readers only ever see "notebook".
_Avoid_: archive, blog, journal

**Notebook entry**:
One dated item in the notebook.
_Avoid_: post, update, archive entry

**Margin note**:
A notebook entry attached to a passage in a chapter by quoting that passage exactly. It is shown beside the text in handwriting.
_Avoid_: annotation, footnote, comment

## Reading

**Reading place**:
The chapter a reader last opened, and whether they reached its end. Used to offer "Continue reading".
_Avoid_: bookmark, progress

**Furthest read**:
The highest chapter a reader has reached the end of. It only ever goes up, and the reader can also set it by hand. It decides what the Companion reveals.
_Avoid_: progress, reading place, current chapter

**Companion**:
The spoiler-safe guide to the book's people and places, written as public site content and never generated from the author's private canon.
_Avoid_: wiki, codex, lore pages

**Companion entry**:
One page in the Companion. Each entry is a **Person**, a **Place** or a **Thing** (an organisation, technology or term). A whole entry, and each fact within it, has a reveal chapter.
_Avoid_: article, card, profile

**Reveal chapter**:
The chapter in which a Companion fact first becomes known to readers. A fact is shown only when the reader's furthest read is at or beyond it. Until then it is hidden completely, with no placeholder or hint.
_Avoid_: unlock chapter, spoiler level

**Quote card**:
A shareable image of a line a reader selected from a chapter, typeset on paper and credited to the author, with the chapter it came from.
_Avoid_: screenshot, pull quote
