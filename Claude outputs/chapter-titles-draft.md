## Spoilers kept out on purpose

- None of the hooks mention what Adam was hired to do to Eleanor (ch 4, 19, 21), Nick being an Aldridge, or the Omega watch (ch 8, 15, 19).
- They also leave out Leo leaving Aegis and being blamed for the theft (ch 7, 20), Carol's fake surgery story, Clara's school-fees arrears and the arrests (ch 12, 22, 24).
- **Ch 7:** his contact label, "T for Twa—", would make a punchier title if you want more edge.
- **Ch 23:** "turns everything Leo believed upside down" hints at a reveal. Make it flatter if that feels like too much.
- **Ch 5:** this chapter is short (about 350 words), so there was little to draw on.
